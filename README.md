SideQuest 🗺️

Boredom, meet your match.

SideQuest turns real life into a game. Every week, AI generates a fresh set of challenges, such as meeting someone new, learning a skill, or trying something outside your comfort zone. Complete them, prove it with a photo, and climb the ranks with your friends.

**How it works**
1. Get your weekly quests, freshly generated by AI.
2. Complete a quest in real life and snap a photo.
3. AI checks that your photo actually matches the quest.
4. Earn XP and coins, and your photo joins a shared album with friends who completed the same quest.
5. Compete on the leaderboard and spend coins on boosts.
   
**Features**
🤖 **AI-generated weekly quests**, a new set every week
📸 **AI-verified photo proof** so only real completions count
⭐ **XP and levels** that reward you for trying new things
👥 **Shared albums** with friends who completed the same quest
🏁 **Friends leaderboard** to compete and compare
🛒 **Weekly boost shop** with XP multipliers and quest rerolls, which rotates every week
